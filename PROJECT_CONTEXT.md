# Adventurer's Kit — project context

Paste or attach this at the start of a new conversation. It describes what exists, how it fits together, and what is still open. Kept up to date as features land; see also `CLAUDE.md` (commands, architecture rules) and `Readme.md` (user-facing overview).

## What it is

A small self-hosted web app for running D&D 5e sessions. Everyone at the table opens it in a browser and sees the same live state.

**Rules baseline: D&D 2024** (SRD 5.2). The 2014 rules (SRD 5.1) are kept as the extra/legacy option: shared SRD names are plain for 2024 and "Name (2014)" for 2014, new characters and D&D Beyond imports default to 2024, edition pickers list 2024 first. Build new rules features for 2024 first; add 2014 behaviour where it differs and is cheap.

- **Home** (`index.html`): landing page with a card per tool (generated from the tool registry).
- **Combat Tracker** (`combat.html`): initiative order, HP, AC, conditions, death saves, rounds. PCs added from a sheet show its portrait (the client looks it up by `characterId` in `charState` rather than copying the image into combat state, which is broadcast on every HP click). Saved characters are added from here ("Add characters" panel, grouped by campaign with an "Add all" per campaign, with each player's initiative roll); every combatant's initiative is editable.
- **Concentration, condition icons and spell effects** (combat): combatants have `concentration` (spell name, '' = none), `concentrationDc` (CON save due after damage, 0 = none) and `effects: [{ id, name, casterId, concentration }]`. Actions: `setConcentration { id, spell, targets }` (ends the previous concentration and its effects, then adds a `concentration: true` effect to each target; players may only target combatants they can see), `endConcentration { id }`, `resolveConcentration { id, passed }` (failed → ends), `addEffect { id, name, casterId? }`, `removeEffect { id, effectId }`. Rules (`combat/service.js`): damage while concentrating sets `concentrationDc = min(30, max(10, ⌊damage/2⌋))` (temp HP doesn't avoid it); 0 HP, the conditions in `ENDS_CONCENTRATION` (Incapacitated, Paralyzed, Petrified, Stunned, Unconscious) and removing the caster end concentration, removing its effects from every target. Permissions: concentration actions → owner of the combatant; `addEffect` → owner of the target or of `casterId`; `removeEffect` → owner of the target or of the effect's caster. `playerView` blanks `casterId` when the caster is hidden. Client: icons in `public/js/core/effects.js` (🔮 concentration, one emoji per condition, spells by name with ✨ as fallback; `badgesFor(c)`); combat chips with icons, a purple "Concentrating: X" chip, a "CON save DC n" chip with Roll (PCs: d20 + CON mod + proficiency if `saveProf.con`) / Passed / Failed; "+ condition" also offers "Concentrating on…" and "Spell effect…". Casting from a combat row's Actions (`attacks.js` `renderActions` option `cast: { blocked, before, after }`): Cast is disabled under those conditions, a concentration spell while concentrating asks to confirm, and spells with concentration or a non-instantaneous duration open the target dialog (`public/js/core/spellDialog.js`, which builds its `<dialog id="spellDlg">` on first use; styles in `components.css`). The wiring of a combatant's Actions (slots, items, modifier toggles, cast hooks) lives in `public/js/core/combatActions.js`, shared by the Combat Tracker and the map card. Map/TV tokens show the icons as a column on the left edge (`map/render.js` `badgeRow`, 3 per cell of size, then "+n"), listed in the tooltip. Petrified was added to the condition list. Not modelled: War Caster advantage, spell durations/rounds, automatic modifier changes on targets (e.g. Bless's +1d4 is still switched on in their Actions).
- **Character Sheets** (`characters.html`): create, edit and store full character sheets with portraits, grouped into campaigns, import from D&D Beyond or a PDF.
- **DM / players** (`src/core/identity.js`, `public/js/core/{identity,join}.js`): no accounts. On first visit a "Who are you?" overlay (any page) offers *I'm the DM*, each character (from `GET /api/characters/summary`), or *Create a new character* (creates it as a guest, then opens `characters.html#edit=<id>`). The choice is kept in localStorage (`kit.identity`) and sent as the WebSocket query (`?role=dm` / `?role=player&character=<id>`) and REST headers (`X-Kit-Role`, `X-Kit-Character`); no/unknown identity = player without a character. Trust-based on purpose (anyone may pick DM) — the point is that players' browsers never *receive* DM-only data.
  - **Hub**: each socket has an `identity`; frames are built per identity from an optional `snapshotFor(identity)`; actions are checked against an optional `permissions` map (`'anyone'` or `(payload, identity) => bool`). The DM may do everything; **an action with no entry is DM-only**.
  - **Players see** (`combat/service.playerView`, `map/service.playerView`, monsters `playerView`): no `hidden` combatants or their tokens (`activeIndex` −1 while one has the turn); monsters with `alias || name`, a `health` word (Unhurt / Hurt >50% / Bloodied >25% / Near death / Down) and no hp/maxHp/ac/dmNotes; the monster library reduced to `{id, icon}` of visible, un-aliased monsters in the fight; no Monsters page.
  - **Players may** (`combat/permissions.js`, map/characters `index.js`): act on their own character only — its sheet (`updateCharacter`/`deleteCharacter`), combatant (HP, conditions, death saves, initiative, rename, remove, `addCharacterToCombat`), map token, and `nextTurn` when it's their turn; `addCharacter` is open to anyone. Everything else (monsters, campaigns, quick add, sorting, clearing, map size, `setCombatantDm {id, hidden?, alias?, dmNotes?}`) is DM-only. REST: new imports are DM-only (403), Sync is DM or owner.
  - **UI**: `body.is-dm` / `.is-player`; `.dm-only` / `.player-only` helper classes; nav badge to switch; other people's sheets are view-only; combat rows show 👁 hide/reveal and a "DM" section (alias for monsters, private notes) to the DM; `addMonsterToCombat` takes `hidden` ("Add hidden" checkbox).
- **Items** (`items.html`, feature `items`, wire `itemState`/`items`, saved to `items.json`): weapons (category, melee/ranged, `damage` dice + type, `versatileDamage`, `range` "normal/long", `properties`, `magicBonus`, `extraDamage` + type), armor (`armorType` light/medium/heavy/shield, `baseAc` — the bonus for shields, `maxDex` null = unlimited, `strengthReq`, `stealthDisadvantage`), gear (`effectDice` + `effect`, e.g. healing), plus cost/weight/notes. Dice are validated/normalised by `normaliseDice` (`NdX[+/-M]` with standard dice, or a flat number; invalid input keeps the old value). The library is seeded from the SRD (`src/srd/items.json`, see *SRD 5.1 + 5.2 content* below) when `items.json` doesn't exist, and new SRD versions are merged in once (a damaged file is left alone). `source` ('srd'/'custom') and `createdBy` ('dm' or a character id) are set by the server. Everyone receives the library; `addItem {id, patch}` needs a character (or DM); players update/delete only items they created; the DM anything. To-hit isn't stored on items — it depends on the wielder (ability mod + proficiency + `magicBonus`); used by character inventories and actions (below).
- **Inventory, Auto AC, actions** (character sheet *Equipment* and *Actions* tabs; combat rows): characters have `inventory: [{ id, itemId, name, qty, equipped }]` (library items; `name` is a fallback copy), `acAuto`, and `weaponProf: { simple, martial }` (D&D Beyond imports map `simple-weapons`/`martial-weapons`). Edited through `updateCharacter` like any sheet field, so the ownership rules apply. `characters/derived.js` `computeAc` (no armor 10+DEX; light +DEX; medium +DEX capped by `maxDex`; heavy no DEX; + shield; + `magicBonus`) — the characters slice and `addCharacterToCombat` use `withDerived`, and item edits rebroadcast characters. Characters saved before this, and imports, keep a manual AC (`acAuto: false`); new ones start on Auto; switching Auto off keeps the shown value. Client: `public/js/core/attacks.js` `buildActions` (equipped weapons: STR melee / DEX ranged / better of both for finesse; to hit = mod + proficiency if proficient with the category + magic; damage = dice + mod + magic, + extra damage, versatile alternative; gear with effect dice → Use; always an Unarmed Strike) and `renderActions` (Attack / Damage / 2-handed / Use buttons; a natural 20 arms a crit — doubled dice — for that action's next damage roll; Use spends one from the inventory). `public/js/core/dice.js` parses/rolls. Rolls are local to the roller's screen (not shared yet). Equipping body armor or a shield unequips the previous one. The old free-text `equipment` field stays as "Other equipment".
- **Roll my own dice** (top-bar toggle **🎲 Auto dice / ✋ My dice**, `core/nav.js`; per browser, `localStorage` `kit.manualDice`, off by default): every die goes through `core/dice.js` `rollDie(sides, label)`. Rolls that the player makes run inside `rollBatch(title, fn)`: automatic mode just runs `fn`; manual mode runs `fn` once with a recording source (missing dice return their max as a placeholder), asks for all of them in one dialog (`core/diceDialog.js`, registered via `setDiceAsker`; numbers 1–sides validated, "Roll the rest for me", Cancel/Esc → `RollCancelled` and nothing is spent), then replays `fn` with the answers. Great Weapon Fighting (2014) rerolls come from a separate `reroll` queue and are asked in a second round. A batch must not branch on its own dice otherwise — a spell attack and its damage are two batches (a crit doubles the damage dice). Covered: weapon Attack / Damage, item Use, Cast (attack, save, heal, auto damage) in `core/attacks.js` (`rolled()`; results shown on every copy of the action via `.action-result[data-key]`, since the list may redraw meanwhile), the concentration save Roll (combat), and spending Hit Dice on a short rest. Not covered: initiative (already typed into the Add-characters box, or rolled by the server when left empty) and the server-side monster initiative. On the map card, clicking inside it pins it so dialogs don't close it.
- **Monster actions** (DM only; `public/js/core/monsterActions.js`): `parseMonsterActions(notes)` (pure) reads the stat block from its first `— Actions —` heading on (sections Actions / Bonus actions / Reactions / Legendary actions; lines `Name: text`, continuation lines appended). Per entry: attack (`Melee/Ranged (Weapon|Spell) Attack: +N to hit` 2014, `… Attack Roll: +N` 2024), damage from the first sentence after `Hit:` (`X (dice) type damage`, flat `N type damage`; "plus"/"and" add, a comma or "or" starts an alternative labelled from the next words — Two-handed / Ranged / Melee / "With shillelagh"…; a "plus" after alternatives adds to all), saving-throw abilities (`DC N Ability saving throw` / `Ability Saving Throw: DC N`, damage from the failure sentence, `half` from "half as much damage" / "Success: Half damage"), and `(Recharge N–6)` in the name or text. On all 675 SRD creatures: 973 attacks (6 without damage — effect-only), 290 save abilities. `renderMonsterActions` shows each with its text and buttons: Attack (d20 + to hit, nat 20 arms a crit for that entry's next Damage), one Damage button per alternative, Recharge (d6) — all through `rollBatch`, so "roll my own dice" works. Wired through `core/combatActions.js` (`monsterFor` in the context; only when `isDm` and the monster has parsed actions): the Combat Tracker monster rows get an Actions section, the map card a pinned DM card. The Monsters page shows them too: each card has a one-line summary of its main actions (`actionSummary`: up to 3 from "Actions", Multiattack left out, `+N` to hit / `DC N`, ⟳ = recharge), and the open monster's panel has an **Actions** section with the roll buttons (redrawn only when its name/notes change; the notes textarea now allows 12000 characters like the server). Its icon circle shows the creature emoji when there's no uploaded icon. `scripts/build-srd.js` appends the 5e-bits `usage` to action names — ` (Recharge 5–6)` / ` (Recharge 6)`, ` (3/Day)`, ` (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)` — unless the source name already has it; SRD rebuilt 2026-11-17 from 5e-bits/5e-database `main` @ a6212beb4b (only `monsters.json` changed: 267 usage labels, 153 recharge abilities). `monsters/service.js` `refreshStatBlocks()` (on load) gives library monsters the new stat block when their notes equal the SRD one apart from those suffixes (never edited); edited notes are kept.
- **Creature icons** (`public/js/core/creatureIcons.js`): a monster without an uploaded icon shows an emoji instead of its initial — `creatureIcon({ name, creatureType })` matches name keywords (ordered regex list: owlbear 🦉 before bear, undead before giants…, "(2024)" and trailing numbers ignored), then the creature type (`BY_TYPE`), else 👾. `glyphFor(c, monster)` (also `portraits.js` `pics.glyphFor(c)`) is used by map/TV tokens (`tokenNode` option `glyph`, `.ini.emoji` sized to the cell), combat rows, the map card, the TV bar, and the monster lists (Monsters page, combat "Add monsters"). Players see a disguised monster's emoji from its alias. Uploaded icons still win.
- **D&D Beyond inventory → library** (`imports/dndbeyond/inventory.js`, pure `planInventory(data, library, newId)`): each D&D Beyond item is matched to the library by normalised name (parentheticals dropped; "Crossbow, Light" ↔ "Light Crossbow"; "Leather" ↔ "Leather Armor"; ammo bundles like "Arrows (20)" convert the count) and goes into `inventory` with D&D Beyond's `equipped` and quantity. Unmatched weapons/armor are created as custom library items (`createdBy: 'dm'`) from the definition (`damage.diceString`, `damageType`, `properties` incl. Versatile notes, `categoryId`, `attackType`, `range`/`longRange`, `armorTypeId`, `armorClass`, `stealthCheck`, `strengthRequirement`, `grantedModifiers` magic / armor-class bonus; generic magic weapons without dice fall back to the base weapon named in `type`), deduped within an import and reused by name later. Other unmatched gear becomes the free-text `equipment`. Import and **Sync** both do this (Sync replaces the inventory: `refreshImported(..., { replaceInventory: true })`). New items are broadcast via `onItemsChanged`. Field names are from the unofficial JSON — unit-tested with hand-written fixtures (`test/ddb-inventory.test.js`); the live import was tested by the user (2026-11-16).
- **Spells** (`spells.html`, feature `spells`, wire `spellState`/`spells`, saved to `spells.json`; seeded and merged from `src/srd/spells.json` like items): `{ level 0–9, school, castingTime, range, components, duration, concentration, ritual, classes, mechanic: attack|save|auto|heal|none, saveAbility, saveSuccess half|none, dice + damageType, addMod, extraDice + extraDamageType, higherLevelDice (per slot level above), cantripScaling (×2/×3/×4 dice at character level 5/11/17), description }`. Items and spells share `src/core/library.js` (`createLibrary`: seed-once, `source`/`createdBy`, damaged file never replaced). Same permissions as items (`addSpell` needs a character; players edit their own).
  - **Characters** gain `spellAbility`, `spellSlots` (9 × `{max, used}`) and `spells: [{ id, spellId, name, prepared }]` (imported from D&D Beyond — see below). Sheet **Spells** tab (`editor/spellbook.js`): ability (shows spell attack = mod + prof, save DC = 8 + mod + prof), slot grid with clickable pips, "Long rest — restore slots", known spells grouped by level with *prepared* toggles.
  - **Actions** (`core/attacks.js`): cantrips + prepared spells, each with a slot picker (levels with slots, showing how many are left) and **Cast**: spends the slot (`onSpendSlot`) and rolls what the spell needs — attack + damage (nat 20 doubles dice), "DC X ABIL save" + damage, healing + modifier, or auto-hit damage. `spellDice` handles cantrip scaling and upcasting. `core/dice.js` parses mixed expressions ("2d8+4d6+3") with `addDice`/`scaleDice`.
- **D&D Beyond spells → library** (`imports/dndbeyond/spells.js`, pure `planSpells(data, library, newId)`): reads `classSpells[].spells` and every list in `spells` (race/class/feat/item/background), matches library spells by name (case-insensitive), creates unknown ones as custom library spells from the definition (`level`, `school`, `activation`, `range` incl. AoE, `components` 1/2/3 = V/S/M, `duration`, `concentration`, `ritual`, `requiresAttackRoll` / `requiresSavingThrow` + `saveDcAbilityId`, damage `modifiers` with `die.diceString` + `subType`, a second damage type as extra dice, heal = `bonus`/`hit-points` with `usePrimaryStat`, `atHigherLevels` `spellscale` → per-level dice, `characterlevel` → cantrip scaling, "half as much damage" in the description → half on save). Prepared = D&D Beyond's `prepared`/`alwaysPrepared`, or always for cantrips, known casters (bard/ranger/sorcerer/warlock) and non-class sources. **Slots are computed** (D&D Beyond doesn't store totals) from the PHB multiclass caster table: full casters + half (paladin/ranger from 2nd, rounded up single-class / down multiclass; artificer rounds up) + third casters (Eldritch Knight / Arcane Trickster); warlock pact slots are added at their slot level; `used` from `spellSlots`/`pactMagic`. `spellAbility` from the highest-level class with `spellCastingAbilityId` (fallback by class name). Import and Sync both apply it (Sync: `replaceSpells: true`). Unit-tested with hand-written fixtures (`test/ddb-spells.test.js`); the live import was tested by the user (2026-11-16).
- **SRD 5.1 + 5.2 content** (`src/srd/`, generated by `scripts/build-srd.js` / `npm run build:srd -- <dir>` from 5e-bits/5e-database JSON; attribution in `NOTICE.md`; *never* copy from 5e.tools or the paid books): 658 spells, 1,016 items (74 weapons, 26 armors, gear/tools/mounts, magic items as gear with rarity + text, healing dice parsed), 675 creatures (full stat block in `notes`, `creatureType`, `cr`, `edition`). Shared names (since 2026-11-17, 2024 baseline): 2024 keeps the plain name, 2014 becomes "Name (2014)"; 2024 entries listed first; names are unique per list (before, 2014 was plain and 2024 "Name (2024)"). 2014 spells use structured `dc`/`damage_at_slot_level`/`heal_at_slot_level`; 2024 records are parsed from the standard SRD wording (saving throw ability, "half as much damage", "increases by XdY for each slot level above", "Cantrip Upgrade", healing "equal to XdY plus your spellcasting ability modifier", second damage type "… damage and … damage"). Reaction/bonus-action triggers move into the description. `src/srd/version.json` holds a content hash: `createLibrary({ starterVersion })` seeds a fresh install, and on a new version merges once (`core/library.js`: SRD entries matched by edition + name without the " (2014)"/" (2024)" suffix — `srdKey`, `nameKey` ignoring case/punctuation/"feet"/trailing "armor" — so the 2024-baseline rename keeps each id with its edition; otherwise by `nameKey` against custom entries or SRD entries saved without an edition, never against an SRD entry of the other edition; each existing entry matched at most once; `source: 'srd'` entries updated in place keeping ids and taking the new name; custom entries untouched; marker file `<name>.srd-version`). The monster library isn't merged: `monsters/service.js` `renameForEditions()` (on load) renames SRD-derived monsters (with an `edition`) whose name is the SRD base name ± suffix to the current SRD name, unless that name is taken. Monsters stay opt-in via **+ SRD creatures** (`addStarterMonsters`, CR order). Items/spells/monsters gained an `edition` field and edition filters; Monsters page has search/type/edition filters (renders the first 120 matches); combat "Add monsters" has a search (first 30). WebSocket `perMessageDeflate` is on (connect payload ~2.6 MB raw → ~0.46 MB). D&D Beyond inventory mapping now only matches weapons/armor to library items of the same type (the SRD's dice-less "Flame Tongue" magic item vs a flame tongue longsword → created as "Flame Tongue (Longsword)").
- **Attack modifiers** (feats, fighting styles, class features, weapon mastery, Hex/Bless…): characters have `modifiers: [{ id, preset, active, only, custom }]` (validated in `characters/model.js`; `custom` = `{ name, toHit, damage: number|PROF|STR…, dice, diceType, appliesTo: weapon|spell|any, range, toggle, note }`). The catalog is data in `public/js/core/modifier-presets.json`, read by the browser (`modifiers.js` loads it with a top-level `await fetch`) and by the server (`characters/derived.js`, for AC). The engine is client-side in `public/js/core/modifiers.js` (both editions labelled; `effectsFor(attack, env)` → to-hit, to-hit dice, flat damage, extra dice parts, reroll rule, crit range, Brutal Critical dice, notes with `{MOD}/{PROF}/{DC}/{TYPE}` filled; class-level scaling from the class text, e.g. "Rogue 5 / Fighter 2"; `unarmedOverride` for Martial Arts). `core/attacks.js` uses it for weapon attacks (per grip: one-handed vs two-handed/versatile, so Dueling vs Great Weapon Fighting apply correctly), the unarmed strike and spells (Agonizing Blast, Empowered Evocation, Elemental Adept, Hex on spell attacks). `dice.js` `roll` supports `reroll` ('gwf-2014' reroll 1–2 once, 'gwf-2024' 1–2 count as 3, 'ea' 1 counts as 2) and `critExtraDice`. Weapons have `mastery` (from SRD 5.2, also on the same 2014 weapons); the Weapon Mastery preset shows each weapon's mastery effect (Graze damage, Topple DC…), limited by `only`. UI: Actions tab "Modifiers" (catalog picker by category, On/Off for situational ones, "only for", custom form); combat row Actions show quick on/off chips (`onToggleModifier`). D&D Beyond (`imports/dndbeyond/modifiers.js`): names from feats, class + subclass features up to the class level, options (fighting styles, invocations) and racial traits map to presets (`BY_NAME`; edition from `detectEdition`: 2024 unless D&D Beyond marks a class or the species as legacy (`isLegacy: true`) and there's no Weapon Mastery); import adds them, Sync merges new ones and keeps existing modifiers/states (situational ones start off, Sneak Attack on). A test checks every detectable key exists in the client catalog. **AC effects**: presets with `ac` — `{ bonus, requiresArmor }` (Defense +1 with armor; Shield +5, Shield of Faith +2, Haste +2 as switches) or an unarmored base `{ base, add: [abilities], noShield }` (Unarmored Defense barbarian 10+DEX+CON / monk 10+DEX+WIS no shield, Draconic Resilience 2014 13+DEX / 2024 10+DEX+CHA, Mage Armor 13+DEX switch); custom modifiers have an `ac` bonus. `computeAc` (Auto AC only) picks the best base — armor, or without body armor plain 10+DEX vs each unarmored formula — then adds the shield (unless the formula forbids it) and bonuses, and returns a `breakdown` sent as `acBreakdown` (AC tile tooltip). `combat/characterSync.syncCombatantsFromSheets` keeps combatants made from sheets at the sheet's AC and HP (after `updateCharacter`, item edits and imports; those broadcast `combat` too). Modifier-only-for-AC presets have no `when` and are skipped by the attack engine. D&D Beyond detection: Defense, Unarmored Defense per class (features are tagged with their class), Draconic Resilience by edition. Not modelled: two-weapon/off-hand attacks, per-turn limits (shown as notes), AC modifiers with a manual AC.
- **HP sync between sheet and combat** (`combat/characterSync.js`): a PC combatant's HP is the sheet's. `adjustHP` writes the new value to the sheet's `hpCurrent` (`writeHpToSheet`) and returns whether it did, so `alsoBroadcast.adjustHP` resends `characters` only for PCs (the slice carries every portrait; monster HP clicks don't resend it). Sheet edits and imports push `hpCurrent`/`hpMax` into the combatant (clamped to max; healing from 0 clears death saves). Temp HP: combatants have `tempHp`, mirrored with the sheet's `hpTemp` both ways; `adjustHP` damage uses it up first, healing never restores it; `setTempHp {id, value 0–999}` replaces it (temp HP don't stack; same permissions/rebroadcast as `adjustHP`). Players see a PC's `tempHp`, never a monster's. Combat rows show an editable "temp" field (blue when > 0) to whoever controls the combatant, a "+N temp" tag to others. Removing a combatant leaves the sheet at its last HP. Tests: `test/hp-sync.test.js`.
- **Short and long rests** (rules in `characters/rest.js`, pure; UI in `public/js/characters/editor/rest.js`). Character fields: `hitDice` is the total pool as text ("5d10", multiclass "3d10 + 2d6"; parsed per die size d6–d12, the same parser on both sides), `hitDiceUsed {d6,d8,d10,d12}`, `pactMagic {level, count}` (Warlock slots *inside* `spellSlots` that come back on a short rest), `resources: [{id, name, max 0–999, used, recharge: short|long|none}]` (limited-use features), `rulesEdition '2014'|'2024'` (default 2024). Actions (owner or DM; also rebroadcast `combat`, then `syncCombatantsFromSheets`): `spendHitDie {id, die, roll?}` (roll + CON mod, ≥ 0, capped at max HP; `roll` is the die the player's screen showed, else the server rolls), `shortRest {id}` (short-recharge resources, pact slots), `longRest {id}` (HP to max, temp HP 0, all slots and short/long resources, Hit Dice: all back in 2024, half the total (≥ 1, largest dice first) in 2014). HP panel: "Hit Dice 2/3 d10 · 2/2 d8", **Short rest** opens a panel to roll Hit Dice one at a time (log of rolls) and **Finish short rest**; **Long rest** confirms first (the Spells tab's Long rest button does the same full rest). Actions tab: Resources list (pips up to 10 uses, −/+ counter above), add form, rest-rules edition. Spells tab: Pact Magic count + level. Before sending a rest the client `untouch()`es the affected fields so pending-edit tracking doesn't keep stale local values. D&D Beyond: hit dice per class (`classes[].definition.hitDice`, `hitDiceUsed`), `pactMagic` from the warlock level (`planSpells`), resources from `actions.{class,race,feat}[].limitedUse` (`imports/dndbeyond/resources.js`: `maxUses` + stat modifier (`statModifierUsesId`) + proficiency bonus (`useProficiencyBonus`, operator 2 = multiply), `resetType` 1 short / 2 long / 3 dawn→long / other→manual — best-effort, fixture-tested only), `rulesEdition` from `detectEdition` (2024 unless D&D Beyond marks the content as legacy and there's no Weapon Mastery; fix it on the sheet). Inventory and spells take the library entry of the character's edition when both exist (`findInLibrary` ranks same edition, then custom, then the other; spells look up "Name (2014)" first for 2014 characters). Sync keeps spent Hit Dice, the sheet's edition and resource used counts (matched by name), and keeps hand-made resources. Not modelled: exhaustion, resources in the combat row, features that recharge partially (e.g. Arcane Recovery). Tests: `test/rest.test.js`.
- **TV view** (`/tv` → `public/tv.html`, `public/js/tv/main.js`, `css/pages/tv.css`): read-only full-screen battle map for a shared screen (AirPlay "separate display" from a Mac, iPad mirroring, HDMI, casting). Connects with `connectSocket({ query: 'role=player' })` — always the filtered player view, even on the DM's computer. Board without labels scaled to fit both screen dimensions (`--cell`), background `<img>` kept across redraws, tokens via the exported `map/render.js` `tokenNode`; bottom bar with round, turn and initiative order (portraits, health words); Fullscreen API (+ webkit prefix; hidden where unsupported, e.g. iPhone), cursor/controls hidden after 3 s idle, Screen Wake Lock re-acquired on visibility. Map page has a **📺 TV view** button (`window.open('/tv')`).

The card is interactive: it stays open while the pointer moves onto it (250 ms hide delay), a click/tap pins it (again, Esc or a click elsewhere unpins), it isn't rebuilt while a select/input in it has focus, and arrow keys there don't move the selected token. The map page now also receives `itemState`/`spellState`. Built only from the state this browser already has (the server's player filtering still applies). Map tokens drop the native `title` tooltip (`tokenNode` option `card`); `portraits.js` gained `characterFor(c)`.

DM-only WS actions `setBackground {id|''}`, `renameBackground`, `deleteBackground` (removes the file; clears it if selected). Only the id travels over the WebSocket — images load over HTTP. The client stretches it over the cell area under the grid (same `<img>` reused across redraws); "Fit grid to image" sets rows = cols × image aspect. `src/core/sharp.js` is the shared lazy sharp loader. Actions `placeToken { id, x, y }` (place or move; integer, in bounds, existing combatant, one token per cell), `removeToken`, `setMapSize { cols, rows }` (5–50; tokens outside go back to the tray), `clearMap`. `snapshot()` prunes positions of combatants that no longer exist; combat's `removeCombatant`/`clearAll` also broadcast `map`. Client: drag with pointer events (mouse + touch), click a token then a cell, arrow keys / Delete / Esc for the selected token; moves are optimistic, the server echo wins; redraws are held during a drag. Tokens show the combatant's picture via `public/js/core/portraits.js` (character portrait by `characterId`, else monster icon by `monsterId`) — the combat list uses the same lookup. Board tokens (map + TV) carry a small name label (`render.js` `nameLabel`, `showName` option of `tokenNode`): a trailing number ("Goblin 3") stays visible while a long name is cut with …; its color follows `healthLevel` (white unhurt → yellow hurt >50% → orange bloodied >25% → red near death; struck through when down; same thresholds as `healthWord`, from the health word for players). With a label the HP bar moves to the token's top edge. The map page has a **Names** checkbox (per browser, `localStorage` `kit-map-names`); the TV always shows names.

All state is shared live over a WebSocket. There are no accounts, no auth and no database.

## Architecture

Server is the single source of truth. Clients send action messages `{ type, payload }`; the server applies them and broadcasts the affected state slice to every client, including the sender (each client gets its own identity-filtered version). One slice per feature:

| Feature | Message |
|---|---|
| combat | `{ type: 'state', state }` |
| characters | `{ type: 'charState', characters }` |
| campaigns | `{ type: 'campaignState', campaigns }` |
| monsters | `{ type: 'monsterState', monsters }` |
| items | `{ type: 'itemState', items }` |
| spells | `{ type: 'spellState', spells }` |
| map | `{ type: 'mapState', map }` |

On every new connection the server sends all of them. Each page ignores the message types it doesn't care about.

### Realtime hub and features

`src/realtime/hub.js` takes a list of **features**. A feature is `{ name, wire: { type, key }, snapshot(), snapshotFor?(identity), actions: { actionType(payload, identity) }, permissions?: { actionType: 'anyone' | (payload, identity) => bool }, alsoBroadcast?: { actionType: [featureNames] } }`. The hub builds an action → feature index (duplicate action names throw at startup), parses and validates incoming messages (non-JSON, non-object and unknown types are ignored), runs the action, then broadcasts that feature's slice plus any `alsoBroadcast` slices (an entry may be a function of the action's return value, e.g. `adjustHP`). Before running an action it checks `permissions` (DM may do anything; no entry = DM-only). REST routes can trigger a broadcast through `hub.broadcast(name)` (the importers via `onCharactersChanged` / `onItemsChanged` / `onSpellsChanged`, the map upload route via `onMapChanged`).

Layering inside a feature: `service.js` owns the state and plain operations; `actions.js` validates payloads and calls the service; `index.js` is the descriptor. Cross-feature dependencies: `combat/actions.js` → `characters/service.js` + `monsters/service.js` (adding them to combat), `combat/characterSync.js` → characters (sheet AC), `map/service.js` → `combat/service.js` (pruning tokens), `campaigns/actions.js` → `characters/service.js` (un-assigning on delete), and `characters/derived.js` → items + `public/js/core/modifier-presets.json` (AC).

### Combat state

```js
state = { round, activeIndex, combatants: [
  { id, name, init, ac, hp, maxHp, tempHp, type: 'pc'|'monster', conditions: [], deathSuccess, deathFail,
    characterId, monsterId,          // '' unless added from a sheet / the monster library
    size,                            // map footprint in cells (1–4), from the stat block for monsters
    hidden, alias, dmNotes }         // DM-only (stripped by playerView)
]}
```

Actions: `addCombatant`, `addCharacterToCombat { characterId, init? }` (PC from a saved sheet: AC, current/max HP; `init` is the roll result, omitted → server rolls d20 + DEX mod; ignored if that character is already in combat; combatant keeps `characterId`), `setInitiative { id, init }` (clamped −10..99, re-sorts keeping the active combatant), `removeCombatant`, `renameCombatant`, `adjustHP`, `addCondition`, `removeCondition`, `toggleDeathSave`, `setTempHp`, `nextTurn`, `sortInit`, `clearAll`, plus `addMonsterToCombat` and `setCombatantDm` (see Monsters / DM sections above). Combatant ids are generated server-side. Adding a combatant re-sorts by initiative while keeping the currently active combatant active (so `activeIndex` can shift on add).

### Character state

```js
{ id, portrait,               // portrait: base64 data URL ('' if none)
  name, race, class, level, background, alignment,
  ac, speed, hitDice,
  abilities: { str, dex, con, int, wis, cha },
  hpMax, hpCurrent, hpTemp,
  saveProf:  { str: bool, ... },
  skillProf: { 'Stealth': bool, ... },   // keys are the display skill names
  equipment,                  // newline-separated text ("Other equipment"); "Dagger x2" = quantity 2
  features,                   // newline-separated text, one entry per line
  notes,
  campaignId, ddbId,          // '' = none
  inventory, acAuto, weaponProf,           // see Inventory, Auto AC, actions
  hitDiceUsed, pactMagic, resources, rulesEdition,   // see Short and long rests
  spellAbility, spellSlots, spells,        // see Spells
  modifiers }                              // see Attack modifiers
// Broadcast with derived fields from characters/derived.js (computed AC, acBreakdown).
```

Actions: `addCharacter { id }` (**id is generated by the client** so the editor can open immediately), `updateCharacter { id, patch }` (partial; `abilities`, `saveProf`, `skillProf` merged key by key; everything goes through `sanitizeCharacterPatch`: level 1–20, ability scores 1–30, name 60 chars, text fields 4000 chars, HP ≥ 0), `deleteCharacter { id }`. (Adding to combat is a combat action now, see above.)

Portrait validation: only `data:image/(jpeg|png|webp);base64,...` up to `MAX_PORTRAIT_CHARS` (derived from `config.MAX_PORTRAIT_BYTES` = 200 KB of image, ≈273k characters), or `''` to clear.

### REST import routes

- `POST /api/import/dndbeyond` — JSON `{ input }` (character URL or numeric id). Fetches `https://character-service.dndbeyond.com/character/v5/character/{id}` (unofficial; sharing must be **Public**), maps it, adds the character, broadcasts, responds `{ character }`. Errors respond `{ error }` (400 bad input, 502 non-OK from D&D Beyond, 404 unusable/private, 500 unexpected). Non-OK and unusable responses are now logged with status and the first 300 chars of the body.
- **Ability scores** (`mapper.js` `abilityModifiers` / `ddbStatValue`): `overrideStats` wins; otherwise base `stats` + `bonusStats` + every `type: 'bonus'` `<ability>-score` modifier from `modifiers.race`, `.class`, `.background` (2024 backgrounds' +2/+1), `.feat`, and `.item` only for items that are equipped (and attuned if `canAttune`; matched by `componentId` = `inventory[].definition.id`); `type: 'set'` modifiers (Gauntlets of Ogre Power…) raise the score to at least that value. **2024 rule**: when the background gives increases — `modifiers.background` score bonuses, or a feat named "<Background> Ability Score Improvements" (matched via `componentId` = the feat's definition id) — the `modifiers.race` score bonuses are skipped: D&D Beyond lists both (a 2024 species can carry an "Ability Score Increases" trait; legacy races keep fixed bonuses) but only applies the background's. Verified on a real 2024 Elf / Scribe character (2026-11-17). (Before 2026-11-17 only feat bonuses were counted.)
- **Avatars:** the mapper returns `avatarUrl` (`data.decorations.avatarUrl`, else `data.avatarUrl`); `client.fetchAvatar` fetches it only from `https://*.dndbeyond.com`, asks the CDN for 256×256 (`width/height/fit=crop/quality` query params — often ignored), then always resizes with `sharp` to a 256×256 JPEG (cover crop, q85, EXIF-rotated, transparency flattened to white). Any `image/*` sharp can decode is accepted; downloads over 15 MB are refused; the result must still fit `MAX_PORTRAIT_BYTES` (200 KB). Avatar tests are skipped when sharp can't load (Node < 20.9). Any failure just means no portrait (logged), never a failed import. Sync only fills the portrait if the character has none. If avatars never arrive, check the logs for "D&D Beyond avatar: skipped".
- `POST /api/import/dndbeyond/refresh` — JSON `{ characterId, input? }`. Re-imports into an existing character (the editor's "Sync from D&D Beyond" button, e.g. after leveling up). Uses the character's stored `ddbId` (saved on import); `input` links/re-links one. Replaces stats/proficiencies/equipment/features via `characters/service.refreshImported`, keeping portrait, notes, temp HP and damage taken (`hpMax - hpCurrent`). 404 unknown character, 400 no D&D Beyond id, otherwise same errors as above.
- `POST /api/import/pdf` — multipart field `file` (max 15 MB). Fillable form fields are matched against alias tables (case/punctuation-insensitive); unmatched non-empty fields go to Notes. Level is parsed from a combined `ClassLevel` field when there's no level field ("Fighter 3 / Wizard 2" sums to 5). No form fields → raw extracted text into Notes. Responds `{ character, warning }`.

Both importers create the character via `characters/service.addImported` using the same sanitizer as normal edits; the page then opens the new character's editor for review. Keep mappers pure (no network/files) so they stay testable.

## Frontend behavior worth knowing

- Shared pieces: `core/socket.js` (reconnecting WebSocket, handlers keyed by message type), `core/nav.js` renders the top nav from `core/tools.js` into `<nav class="app-nav" data-active="<id>">`, the same registry builds the home cards. To add a tool: add a registry entry, a page, `js/<id>/`, `css/pages/<id>.css`, and a server feature.
- **characters**: a player with a character lands on their own sheet's editor (once per page load, `openOwnSheet` in `characters/main.js`; "Back to list" still shows everyone); the DM and guests land on the list. List view (cards with avatar, name, race/class, level pill, AC/HP/speed, HP bar), import panel (D&D Beyond tab, PDF tab with drag-and-drop), editor view (hero panel, vitals tiles, HP panel, tabs Stats / Equipment / Actions / Spells / Features / Notes). Modifiers, saves, skill bonuses, proficiency bonus and initiative are computed client-side, never stored.
- Text edits are debounced 300 ms before sending; the server echoes full state to everyone.
- **Pending-edit tracking** (`characters/store.js`: `touch()` / `mergePending()`): fields touched in the last 2.5 s keep their local value when new state arrives; focused fields are never overwritten. This fixed a real race where an echo from an earlier edit overwrote newer typing. Keep it when changing the editor.
- Portraits are cropped to a centered square and resized to 256 px JPEG in the browser. They are part of the character object, so every `charState` broadcast carries all portraits — fine at table scale.

**Design system** — D&D Beyond-inspired: dark top nav with a red underline on the active page, red accent (`--wax`), amber secondary (`--gold`), Roboto Condensed uppercase headings, Roboto body (Google Fonts), light/dark following the OS (`prefers-color-scheme`, `data-theme` overrides). Tokens live in `css/tokens.css` (`--bg`, `--bg-panel`, `--ink`, `--ink-soft`, `--line`, `--wax`, `--gold`, `--good`, `--blue`, `--nav-bg`); `base.css` and `components.css` are shared; each page has its own `css/pages/*.css`. Pages link the three shared sheets plus their own.

## Known gaps and caveats

- The D&D Beyond endpoint is unofficial and can change without notice; the live import was tested by the user (2026-11-16). Non-OK responses are logged (`docker compose logs`).
- D&D Beyond **AC is a best-effort estimate** from equipped armor and shield; skill/save proficiencies come from scanning `modifiers` arrays and may miss sources. The import adds a note saying so.
- PDF alias table is heuristic; the official sheet's exact field names were not verified. Flattened/scanned PDFs only yield raw text.
- No authentication: DM/player roles are trust-based (anyone may pick DM); they only keep DM-only data off players' screens.
- Mobile: every page was checked at 360–390 px (headless Firefox screenshots, 2026-11-16). Combat rows have a phone layout (`@media (max-width: 600px)` in `combat.css`: Visible/Remove under the row, −5/−1/+1/+5 buttons ≥ 36 px, temp HP + bar on a second line). Other pages had no horizontal overflow; the nav, sheet tabs and battle map scroll sideways on purpose (map cells ≥ 32 px, tokens are `touch-action: none`). Items and Spells render the whole library (~1,000 items → a very long page on phones); Monsters caps at 120. The TV view is meant for landscape screens.
- Fonts come from Google Fonts at runtime; offline they fall back to system fonts.
- Client scripts are ES modules, so the pages need to be served over HTTP (not opened from disk).
