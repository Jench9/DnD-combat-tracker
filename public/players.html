Players

Who can log in, and which characters each person plays
Only the admin and DMs (at a table they run) can manage accounts.
This server runs without accounts (KIT_AUTH=open): anyone who opens it picks who they are. Accounts are the default; remove KIT_AUTH=open to use them.
Invite someone

Creates a link to send to one person (by chat, email…). They choose a username and password, then play in the campaigns and characters you tick — and run (DM) the campaigns ticked under Runs, if any. The link works once, for 7 days.
