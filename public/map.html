You can move your own token. Drag a token onto the grid, or click it and then click a cell. With a token selected: arrow keys move it, Delete puts it back in the tray, Esc deselects. Hover over a token (or tap it) to see its details. Pinch with two fingers to zoom and move them together to pan (Ctrl + wheel on a computer).
